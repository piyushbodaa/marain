Privacy Policy for Marain

Effective Date: November 22, 2026 App Package: app.marain.learn Developer: Piyush Bodaa
Plain-English Summary: Marain is a 100% offline Android educational app. It requires no account, shows no ads, includes no analytics or tracking SDKs, and never collects, transmits, sells, or shares any personal or device data.

1. Zero Data Collection

Marain (app.marain.learn), developed by Piyush Bodaa (https://piyushbodaa.com), is designed from the ground up to work completely offline on your Android device.

  • No account or sign-in: You do not provide your name, email address, phone number, or any login credentials.
  • No personal or device data collected: We do not collect, transmit, store on remote servers, sell, or share any personal information, identifiers, location data, or usage telemetry.
  • No advertisements: Marain contains zero ads and zero advertising SDKs.
  • No third-party analytics or crash reporters: Marain does not bundle third-party tracking, analytics, telemetry, or crash-reporting libraries.

2. On-Device Local Storage Only

To remember your learning journey between sessions, Marain stores your app state strictly locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences. This local-only data includes:

  • Completed lessons, practice drill scores, and unlocked milestones
  • CEFR fluency rank (A1–C2), earned XP, daily XP goal progress, hearts, and learning streaks
  • Mastered phrasebook flashcards and saved chapter bookmarks
  • Reading preferences (Light Paper / Warm Sepia / Dark Obsidian theme and S / M / L font size)

This data never leaves your phone. You can erase it at any time by clearing the app’s storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling Marain, which deletes all local app data automatically.

3. Permissions & Network Access

All lessons, practice drills, and phrasebook entries are bundled directly inside the app. Marain does not require internet access to function and does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or external storage.

4. Children’s Privacy

Marain is safe for learners of all ages. Because the app operates completely offline and collects zero personal information from any user, we do not knowingly collect any personal data from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction).

5. Changes to This Policy

If Marain ever introduces optional online features in a future release, this Privacy Policy will be updated clearly before those changes take effect, along with an updated effective date at the top of this page.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or Marain (app.marain.learn), please reach out to the developer, Piyush Bodaa, via https://piyushbodaa.com.
